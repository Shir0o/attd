## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where is my data stored?
All attendance data is stored locally on your device. If you enable Google Drive backup, the app only accesses its own dedicated application folder in your personal Google Drive.

### How do I back up or restore my data?
Open **Settings → Google Drive Sync** to configure manual or automatic backups. You can also use **Settings → Backup to Local Storage** to create a local backup file.

### How do I export reports?
If **Settings → Enable Report Export** is turned on, you can generate reports and export them to Google Sheets from the reporting section.

### Why is Google Drive Sync not working?
Make sure you are signed in with the Google account you want to use for backups, and check that you have a network connection. If the issue persists, try signing out and signing back in from the settings page.

### How do I delete my data?
You can delete individual records from within the app, or clear all app data by removing the app's storage in your device settings. Cloud backups can be deleted from the application data folder in your Google Drive.
