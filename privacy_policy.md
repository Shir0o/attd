# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 16, 2026

Thank you for choosing **Attendance Tracker**. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Data We Collect

### Attendance Data
The core functionality of the app involves managing member lists and recording attendance for events. This data (including names, event titles, and dates) is **stored locally on your device**. We do not have access to this data unless you choose to sync it with your own Google Drive.

### Handoff Files
If you choose **Hand off** on an event, the app creates a plain, unencrypted file containing the event name, the session date and the roster names, and opens your device's share sheet. You decide whether and to whom to send it; the app sends nothing itself and we do not receive it. A person who receives the file can send back a file of the marks they recorded, which you open and review before anything is added. If you open a handoff file from someone else, its event, roster names and your marks stay on your device (and in your own Google Drive backup if you have enabled it) until you remove the event.

### Usage & Diagnostic Data
This app operates 100% locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or monitor stack traces, crash reports, or diagnostic usage data via third-party services like Firebase Crashlytics.

## 2. Google Services & Permissions

### Google Sign-In
We use Google Sign-In to authenticate your identity for cloud features. We only access your basic profile information (email and name) to facilitate the connection.

### Google Drive Sync
If you enable the backup/sync feature:
- **Scope:** We use the `drive.appdata` scope. This is a restricted, "hidden" folder in your Google Drive specifically for this application.
- **Access:** The app **cannot** see, read, or modify any other files in your personal Google Drive.
- **Control:** You retain full control over these backup files.

### Google Sheets Integration
If you export reports to Google Sheets, the app will request permission to create and update specific spreadsheets.

## 3. Third-Party Sharing
We **do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer** your attendance data to third parties. The only time attendance data leaves your device other than to your own Google account (Drive backup, Sheets export) is when you choose to send a handoff file to someone. Diagnostic data is processed by Google (Firebase) solely for the purpose of app maintenance.

## 4. Data Retention & Deletion
- **Local Data:** You can delete all app data by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by deleting individual records within the app.
- **Cloud Data:** You can delete your sync files by disconnecting your Google account and trashing the application data folder in your Google Drive.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
